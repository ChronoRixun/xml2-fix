XML2 Fix 1.9.0 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.4.1 (the port's beta 8), with Ultimate Legends 0.4.0 or
later. It needs the matching builder: rebuild with Legends Classic 0.4.1-beta.1 or later, then play. A 1.9.0 game and
a 1.8.0 game (beta 7 or the stable) refuse each other at join; saves carry over, the format is unchanged.

Everything below is new in beta 8 and **has not been played yet**: each item passed its native tests against the
game's own code (and, where it says so, against the first game's code side by side), and each needs a live check
against the original before the stable release.

- **Beta 8: the first game's bosses.** The colosseum dopples throw the fastball special and set off the arena's
  traps again, and Avalanche, Pyro, Mystique, Shadow King, Magneto and Blob get their own X-Men Legends brains back
  (Marrow's was already the PC's own). Master Mold is unchanged. Gameplay rule 40.
- **Beta 8: guns aim and hit like the first game's.** A bullet that passes beta 7's half-second hit timer now flies
  along the first game's thin ray from the gun's muzzle and stops at the first thing in its way, instead of the
  PC's wide beam. Enemy power picks also run during locked moves for every enemy type, the Advanced Sentinel and
  Juggernaut get their first-game classes, and Danger Room enemies flinch on every hit again. Gameplay rule 16
  revision 14.
- **Beta 8: damage rounds like the first game.** A hit becomes a whole number after the defense and after the
  scale, and no hit does more than 32,000 (gameplay rule 41).
- **Beta 8: moves the first game had.** Wall cling and back-flip for the acrobats (rule 39), Rogue's drain decision
  (rule 43), Might rank 3 lifting the first game's heavy objects (rule 38), and seven shared moves - ice and lava
  skating, Nightcrawler's teleport landings, popups and their landings, the charge, Nightcrawler's buddy teleport and
  Gambit's object charge - as X-Men Legends played them (rule 42).
- **Beta 8: room for graphics mods.** The model-file cache grows from 200 to 1024 entries (with 2048 resource names
  and 8192 model handles), so a heavier mod no longer bounces you back to the previous zone (rules 31-32).
- **Beta 8: reviving a hero costs what it did in the first game:** 200 / 800 / 1,800 / 3,200 / 5,000 Tech Bits by
  team level band; beta 7 charged far more (gameplay rule 10 revision 3).
- **Beta 8: what to do.** Update XML2 Fix to 1.9.0-beta.1 and the builder to 0.4.1-beta.1, rebuild, then play.
  Everyone in a co-op game needs the same pair.

Includes everything in 1.8.0 (beta 7): the gunmen's weapon brain with the first game's hit timer, enemy powers and
blocks, the "Alison" label and one-sided walls in the XML1 levels.

**Packaging:** as 1.8.0 - pinned GameNetworkingSockets, protobuf and the project CA linked statically into
`dinput.dll`, checked by `scripts/check_release_dll.py`; Windows system imports only.
