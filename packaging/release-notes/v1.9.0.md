XML2 Fix 1.9.0 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.4.1 (the port's beta 8: content 28, lobby token `LC37`), with
Ultimate Legends launcher 0.4.0 or later. It needs the matching builder: update XML2 Fix to 1.9.0-beta.1 and the
builder to 0.4.1-beta.1, **Rebuild**, then **Play**. A 1.9.0 game and a 1.8.0 game (beta 7 or the stable) refuse each
other at join, so everyone in a co-op game needs the same pair. Saves carry over; the format is unchanged.

**What's new in beta 8**

- **Colossus's charge hits.** It knocks enemies back and does damage, as in the first game (gameplay rule 46).
- **The GRSO Officer and Captain have their guns and grenades** (the builder's fix, also broken in 0.4.0): the
  Officer's nullifier beam and incendiary grenade, the Captain's knockback gun and flashbang.
- **Saved rooms stay cleared after updating.** Beta 8 changed 65 map files without moving anything in them, and the
  save reader used to throw away every saved room of a changed map (enemies, pickups and doors came back). The reader
  now keeps them; in 148 real saves every room keeps at least what it keeps on 0.4.0.
- **Bigger engine limits.** The model-file cache grows from 200 to 1024 entries (with 2048 resource names and 8192
  model handles), the character sound sets from 16 to 127, the animation pool from 500 to 22,528 clips and the resident
  character stats from 31 to 320, and the build's own `XMen2.exe` can use 4 GB of memory instead of 2 GB (your
  install's exe is never changed). That is groundwork for heavier graphics mods.
- **The first game's bosses.** The colosseum dopples throw the fastball special and set off the arena's traps, and
  Avalanche, Pyro, Mystique, Shadow King, Magneto and Blob get their X-Men Legends brains back (rule 40).
- **Juggernaut grabs** you, holds you and hits, as in the first game, and the shared grab, pickup and thrown-landing
  recovery and the flying charge picking up whoever it hits follow X-Men Legends (rule 45).
- **Guns aim like the first game's.** A bullet flies along the first game's thin ray from the muzzle and stops at the
  first thing in its way; the Advanced Sentinel and Juggernaut get their first-game enemy classes, and Danger Room
  enemies flinch on every hit again (rule 16 revision 14).
- **Damage rounds like the first game:** a hit becomes a whole number after the defense and after the scale, and no
  hit does more than 32,000 (rule 41).
- **More of the first game's moves:** wall cling and back-flip for the acrobats (rule 39), Rogue's drain decision
  (rule 43), Might rank 3 lifting the first game's heavy objects (rule 38), ice and lava skating, Nightcrawler's
  teleports, popups, the charge and Gambit's object charge (rule 42), and the jump attack's and flying charge's damage,
  Beast's bounce, the fastball, idle animations and animation speed for scaled characters (rule 46).
- **The pause menu's Objectives page** lists what the first game listed, with no gaps, and opens on the current one.

**Still in progress (next beta):** the first game's telekinesis for Phoenix, its Guard choice (what a Guard press
grabs, picks up or throws, and the acrobats' wall lunge) and the flying carry (Phoenix, Rogue and Storm carrying and
throwing in flight) are switched off in this beta while they are finished. Guard grabs enemies and picks up objects as
in 0.4.0, Phoenix's power 1 is the PC game's telekinesis, and flying, the flying charge and the first game's flight
energy cost are unchanged.

**Tested:** a hand test of the release content covered Colossus's charge, the GRSO grenades, Guard grabbing and
picking up, and saved rooms in a HAARP save. Automated runs on the beta 8 candidates covered the boss fights, Juggernaut's grab, crowded
rooms with the raised limits (no refusals), memory above 2 GB without a crash, and co-op on one PC with two
instances of the game (fights and a level change, no desync; an older build refused by name). Play between separate
PCs has not been tested yet.

**Known issues**

- The flying carry, Phoenix's XML1 telekinesis and the XML1 Guard choice are off (above).
- With a controller connected the game can pause for a moment about once a second (easy to see on the Team
  Management screen). It happens on 0.4.0 too; the cause is found (the game re-checking the controllers) and a fix
  is on the way.
- Blob fights now, but in rare runs he can still stand still.
- From 0.4.0: at mid range the MP5 soldier still does more damage than the original, and the flamethrower and
  lightning commanders have not been compared side by side with the original yet (Legends Classic issue #101).

Includes everything in 1.8.0: online co-op, the classic HUD and Codex, enemies that fight like the first game, every
gunman's weapon brain with the first game's hit timer, one-sided walls in the first game's levels, the "Alison" label
and the first game's revival price.

**Packaging:** as 1.8.0 - pinned GameNetworkingSockets, protobuf and the project CA linked statically into
`dinput.dll`, checked by `scripts/check_release_dll.py`; Windows system imports only.
