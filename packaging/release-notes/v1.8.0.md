XML2 Fix 1.8.0 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.4.0 (content 27, lobby token `LC36`), with Ultimate Legends
launcher 0.4.0. It is the first stable release since 1.7.1 and gathers the seven 1.8.0 betas.

**What's new since 1.7.1**

- **Online co-op.** Host or join a game by code, or browse public games, without port forwarding or ini editing.
  Friends can drop in mid-campaign and come into play at the next level change; during the first game's solo story
  parts the other players stay connected and watch. A guest dropping out of a game of three or four no longer ends
  it for the others, slow players are no longer thrown out, and four-player starts no longer crash. Online runs at
  60 frames a second and no longer drops to about 15 on a busy PC. Connections are encrypted, with a relay when a
  direct connection fails.
- **Fewer crashes.** The team-combo popup crash is fixed and the model pools are four times bigger, so effects, props
  and heads no longer go missing in big fights. Quitting no longer crashes now and then.
- **The classic look.** Legends Classic builds draw the first game's HUD, and the Codex's Details and Back work as in
  the first game. A refused-save message names the save file instead of its whole path.
- **Enemies fight like the first game.** Enemy combos stop when your hit makes them flinch. Every gun enemy uses the
  first game's weapon brain: they shoot from range instead of walking up to punch, and hold their grenades at full
  energy. A gun only lands a hit on the first game's half-second timer. Enemy powers come on time, and enemy power
  attacks are never blocked, as in the original.
- **Walls only block you from the front** in the first game's levels, as in X-Men Legends, so enemies no longer get
  stuck on the ends of walls. The X-Men Legends II maps keep their own walls.
- **"Alison" beside Magma's early lines**, as in the first game.
- **Reviving a hero costs what it did in the first game:** 200 / 800 / 1,800 / 3,200 / 5,000 Tech Bits by team level band; beta 7 charged far more.

The gameplay and look changes are settings the Legends Classic builder writes; without them X-Men Legends II plays
as before.

**How to update.** In Ultimate Legends **0.4.0** (Stable channel), update both parts - **XML2 Fix to 1.8.0** and the
**builder to 0.4.0** - then **Rebuild**, then **Play**. Saves carry over; the save format is unchanged.

**Co-op needs the same version.** Everyone in a session needs XML2 Fix 1.8.0 and a Legends Classic 0.4.0 build.
0.4.0 makes the same game as 0.4.0-beta.7, but 1.8.0 is not the same fix as 1.8.0-beta.7: it corrects the revival
price, so a 1.8.0 PC and a beta 7 PC refuse each other at join, naming `10 Xtraction services/RevivalCost`. Beta 7
players update XML2 Fix to 1.8.0 (the builder too); 0.2.x games and the older betas cannot join. Test mode must be
off for online play.

**Tested:** online was tested on one PC with two instances of the game (Tested online on one PC: two games played together for 21 minutes at 30 fps (fights with AI teammates, a co-op save, a player dropping out and rejoining by room code, two level changes) plus a 3-player drop test at 60 fps, with no desync; beta 6 and stable games refuse each other.). Play between separate
PCs and four-player internet games are not certified yet.

**Known issues**

- **Host loss ends the session.** There is no host migration or automatic reconnection; a guest can join again by
  code. A failed shared-campaign restore can require rehosting. LAG warnings may still show.
- **Public games connect players directly,** so players can see each other's internet address. Use a private code to
  play only with friends.
- **Button prompts** (issue #8): after rebinding the controls, or with Steam Input, a prompt may show the wrong
  button. Not reproduced on Windows so far.
- **Enemies** (Legends Classic issue #101): unarmed enemies still attack about 1.5 times as often as in the first
  game; at mid range the MP5 soldier still does more damage than the original (about 1.6 to 2.2 times); the
  flamethrower and lightning commanders have not been compared side by side with the original yet.
- Legends Classic's other open issues (falls #22, cosmetics #54, loot and shop #6, solo parts online #106) are listed
  in the [builder 0.4.0 release](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/legends-classic/releases/tag/v0.4.0).

- **Beta 7: every gunman fights like the first game.** Beta 6 gave the HAARP officer the first game's weapon brain;
  now all of its gun-carrying enemies have it - MP5 and laser soldiers, the nullifier and knockback officers (who

- **Beta 7: enemy powers and blocks like the first game.** Enemies no longer save up a power while they are busy

  melee attacks about as often as in the original again, and an enemy's power attack is never blocked: in the
  first game it always lands, on the PC about a quarter of them used to be blocked with no damage. This is gameplay
  rule 16 revision 11, so a beta 7 game and a beta 6 game refuse each other at join, naming `16 NpcPowerRule`.
- **Beta 7: "Alison" beside Magma's early lines.** The first game labels the lines Magma speaks before she takes
  her codename "Alison"; the port showed "Magma". With Legends Classic 0.4.0-beta.7 those lines read "Alison", with
  Magma's portrait and talking animation (`[Game] ClassicSpeakers = 1`; display only).
- **Beta 7: walls only stop you from the front, as in the first game.** X-Men Legends' levels only collide with
  the side of a wall that faces you; XML2's movement also hit walls from behind, so characters wedged on wall ends
  (the HAARP officer stood stuck at range). Legends Classic builds now turn on the first game's one-sided walls in
  the first game's levels (`[Game] WorldBackfaces = xml1` with the build's list of XML1 zones, gameplay rule 30); the
  X-Men Legends II maps an install still carries keep XML2's walls. A game with it and one without (or with another
  zone list) refuse each other at join, naming `30 WorldBackfaces`.
- **Beta 7: what to do.** Update XML2 Fix to beta 7 and the builder to 0.4.0-beta.7 in the launcher, then Rebuild
  (the builder refuses a beta 6 fix, which does not know `[Game] ClassicSpeakers` or `[Game] WorldBackfaces` and
  cannot read the new weapon-brain rows). The build's content changes (content 27, lobby token `LC36`), so a beta 7
  game and a beta 6 game never join each other: everyone in a session updates both parts and rebuilds. Saves carry
  over.
- **Beta 6: enemies fight like the first game.** Enemy combos now stop when your hit makes them react, as in

  other at join, naming `16 NpcPowerRule`. **What to do:** update XML2 Fix to beta 6 and the builder to

- **Beta 6: no frame around the small map.** The classic HUD's small-map frame (Legends Classic beta 5) was

- **Beta 5: the classic look.** Legends Classic 0.4.0-beta.5 dresses the port's screens in the first game's art from
  your own disc - the HUD and its small-map frame, the pause menu, Options, the Save/Load lists and dialogs, the
  character room, Review, the Codex, the Danger Room, the Forge, the healer, trivia, loading screens and credits - and
  in the character room the white cones and gold rings are gone. This fix draws the classic HUD (`[Game] ClassicHud =
  xml1`) and makes the Codex's Details open the entry you are on, with Back from a biography returning to the list
  (`[Game] ClassicCodex = 1`). Both only change what you see: no gameplay, online-agreement or save change, and both

  game never join each other: everyone in a session updates and rebuilds. The builder refuses a beta 4 DLL, which
  does not know the two keys.
- **Beta 4: online no longer drops to about 15 frames a second on a busy PC.** Beta 3's 60 fps online mode could
  collapse to 15-20 fps while other games or programs were running, even on a PC that played 30 fine. The cause was
  the game's own wait between online frames: it keeps the processor busy instead of sleeping, so every online game uses
  a whole core, and on a crowded PC Windows keeps pushing it aside for long stretches. Online frames now wait with a
  timer at slightly raised priority instead. On a PC deliberately overloaded to reproduce the problem, beta 3 ran about
  21 frames a second and beta 4 holds 55 to 60, and the game uses about a fifth of a core instead of up to a whole one.
  Nothing else changes: the game's speed, gameplay and saves are the same, and the online agreement (rule 29,
  `[Online] TickRate = 60`) is unchanged. An off-by-default `[Online] TickPriority = thread` raises the whole game
  thread while online; it measured 59.8 fps in the same test but is opt-in until testers have tried it. **What to do:**
  update XML2 Fix to beta 4 in the launcher, then Rebuild, so every PC in a session is on the same build; the 30 fps
  setting still works as before. `[Online] TickWait = spin` brings beta 3's behaviour back if it is ever needed for
  support. The beta line is renumbered from 0.3.0 to 0.4.0; the next stable will be 0.4.0 (online co-op + the classic
  look); nothing else changes for you.
- **Beta 3: online at 60 fps.** Online sessions ran at 30 frames a second; Legends Classic builds now play online
  at 60 like single-player (`[Online] TickRate = 60`; the game's speed is unchanged, upload per player about 11 KB/s).
  A slow PC can set `TickRate = 30` - then everyone in that session needs 30: the rate is gameplay-agreement rule 29,
  so a 60 and a 30 game refuse each other at join, naming `29 TickRate`, and a beta 2 game and a beta 3 game never
  join. Everyone updates XML2 Fix to beta 3 and rebuilds with builder 0.3.0-beta.3.
- **Beta 3: quitting no longer crashes now and then.** Two of the game's controller-detection workers could clean up
  shared data at the same time when you quit; now only the last one does.
- **Beta 3: bigger model pools, cleanup fixed.** With beta 2's bigger pools, a level change or quit could clean up
  some of the larger pools wrongly (an empty slot taken as a model, or old entries left behind). They are now cleaned
  up in full, with new tests that run the game's own cleanup code.
- **Beta 2: the team-combo crash is fixed** (beta 1, hosting alone in the Maintenance Garage): the game makes every
  model from fixed pools, and with one full a team-combo popup lost a hero's head and crashed. Such a popup is now
  hidden (the combo still counts), and **the model pools are four times bigger** in Legends Classic builds
  (`[Engine] ModelPools = x4`: 4096 models, 2048 IGB, 128 actors ...), so effects, props and heads no longer go
  missing in heavy fights. Off by default for plain XML2.
- **Same versions only:** the pools are gameplay-agreement rule 28 (ModelPools) - a full pool refuses spawns - so a
  1.8.0-beta.1 game and a beta.2 Legends Classic game refuse each other at join, naming `28 ModelPools` on the beta.2
  PC. Everyone updates the launcher's XML2 Fix to beta 2 and rebuilds with builder 0.3.0-beta.2.
- **Four-player start is fixed:** participants keep distinct peers when the game starts.
- **Hero swaps survive level changes.** Solo-segment hand-back runs only during the segment and where it ends,
  using the heroes held when the segment began (rule 26 revision 6).
- **Slow players stay connected.** LAG warnings may still show, but the slow-player drop is disabled.
- **Frozen guests:** a joiner frozen for 30 seconds is removed through the agreed cut frame; survivors keep playing.
  A host drop or freeze still ends the joiners' session with "lost connection to the host".

- Host or join online by code, or browse public games, without port forwarding or ini editing. Codes appear in
  the game's online menus and the launcher. Rooms open when you enter Play Online.
- Room tokens are exactly the host's `GameVersion` (`LC33`), so matching public rows can join. The full gameplay
  agreement still checks content and rules on admission.
- Drop-in is **ON by default in Legends Classic 0.3.0**. Start alone; late players wait until the next level change.
  Players without a hero during solo story segments stay connected watching and regain a hero when the party widens.
- When a guest drops abruptly in a game of three or more, survivors agree a cut frame and play on
  (rule 25 GnsTransport revision 4, framing 3). The guest can join again by code. The host keeps hosting after the
  last guest leaves. **Host loss still ends the session; there is no host migration or automatic reconnection.**
- Contract v1.5: `room_closed` means the game cannot take players right now; `room_full` means all four seats are taken.
- Online hardening, encrypted connections and relay fallback. Public games connect directly, so players can see
  each other's internet address. The broker caps each address at two public rooms and two seats in others' rooms.
- Includes 1.7.1's bedroom picture window and per-hit hero stun behavior, and 1.7.0's conversation timing fix.
