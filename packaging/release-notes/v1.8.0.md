XML2 Fix 1.8.0 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.4.0 (content 26 / LC35 from beta 6; beta 5: content 25 / LC34; betas 1-4: content 24 / LC33), with Ultimate Legends 0.4.0.

Update the launcher to **0.4.0 first**, then **Rebuild**, then **Play**. Everyone needs matching builds and fixes;
0.2.x and the 0.4.0 betas cannot join each other. Test mode must be off for online play. Saves carry over; the format is unchanged.


- **Beta 6: enemies fight like the first game.** Enemy combos now stop when your hit makes them react, as in
  X-Men Legends, instead of finishing through it (on the test bench enemies attacked about 1.7 times as often as the
  original; now they match it). The HAARP officer gets the first game's weapon brain: one aimed shot about every
  0.9 seconds, no bursts and no melee from range, he closes in from far away, and at full energy he holds his grenade
  instead of firing. Both are gameplay-agreement rule 16 revision 10, so a beta 6 game and a beta 5 game refuse each
  other at join, naming `16 NpcPowerRule`. **What to do:** update XML2 Fix to beta 6 and the builder to
  0.4.0-beta.6 in the launcher, then Rebuild (the builder refuses a beta 5 fix, which does not know
  `[Game] NpcSemiAuto`). The build's content changes (content 26, lobby token `LC35`), so a beta 6 game and a beta 5
  game never join each other: everyone in a session updates both parts and rebuilds.
- **Beta 6: no frame around the small map.** The classic HUD's small-map frame (Legends Classic beta 5) was
  larger than the map it enclosed and the original game never framed it, so it is gone and the small map is
  drawn where the game itself puts it, as in stock XML2. Nothing else about the classic HUD changes. **What to do:**
  update XML2 Fix and the builder in the launcher, then Rebuild.
- **Beta 5: the classic look.** Legends Classic 0.4.0-beta.5 dresses the port's screens in the first game's art from
  your own disc - the HUD and its small-map frame, the pause menu, Options, the Save/Load lists and dialogs, the
  character room, Review, the Codex, the Danger Room, the Forge, the healer, trivia, loading screens and credits - and
  in the character room the white cones and gold rings are gone. This fix draws the classic HUD (`[Game] ClassicHud =
  xml1`) and makes the Codex's Details open the entry you are on, with Back from a biography returning to the list
  (`[Game] ClassicCodex = 1`). Both only change what you see: no gameplay, online-agreement or save change, and both
  stay off unless the build writes them. A refused-save message now names the save file instead of its whole path,
  which used to run off the box. **What to do:** update XML2 Fix to beta 5 and the builder to 0.4.0-beta.5 in the
  launcher, then Rebuild. The build's content changes (content 25, lobby token `LC34`), so a beta 5 game and a beta 4
  game never join each other: everyone in a session updates and rebuilds. The builder refuses a beta 4 DLL, which
  does not know the two keys.
- **Beta 4: online no longer drops to about 15 frames a second on a busy PC.** Beta 3's 60 fps online mode could
  collapse to 15-20 fps while other games or programs were running, even on a PC that played 30 fine. The cause was
  the game's own wait between online frames: it keeps the processor busy instead of sleeping, so every online game uses
  a whole core, and on a crowded PC Windows keeps pushing it aside for long stretches. Online frames now wait with a
  timer at slightly raised priority instead. On a PC deliberately overloaded to reproduce the problem, beta 3 ran about
  21 frames a second and beta 4 holds 55 to 60, and the game uses about a fifth of a core instead of up to a whole one.
  Nothing else changes: the game's speed, gameplay and saves are the same, and the online agreement (rule 29,
  `[Online] TickRate = 60`) is unchanged. An off-by-default `[Online] TickPriority = thread` raises the whole game
  thread while online; it measured 59.8 fps in the same test but is opt-in until testers have tried it. **What to do:**
  update XML2 Fix to beta 4 in the launcher, then Rebuild, so every PC in a session is on the same build; the 30 fps
  setting still works as before. `[Online] TickWait = spin` brings beta 3's behaviour back if it is ever needed for
  support. The beta line is renumbered from 0.3.0 to 0.4.0; the next stable will be 0.4.0 (online co-op + the classic
  look); nothing else changes for you.
- **Beta 3: online at 60 fps.** Online sessions ran at 30 frames a second; Legends Classic builds now play online
  at 60 like single-player (`[Online] TickRate = 60`; the game's speed is unchanged, upload per player about 11 KB/s).
  A slow PC can set `TickRate = 30` - then everyone in that session needs 30: the rate is gameplay-agreement rule 29,
  so a 60 and a 30 game refuse each other at join, naming `29 TickRate`, and a beta 2 game and a beta 3 game never
  join. Everyone updates XML2 Fix to beta 3 and rebuilds with builder 0.3.0-beta.3.
- **Beta 3: quitting no longer crashes now and then.** Two of the game's controller-detection workers could clean up
  shared data at the same time when you quit; now only the last one does.
- **Beta 3: bigger model pools, cleanup fixed.** With beta 2's bigger pools, a level change or quit could clean up
  some of the larger pools wrongly (an empty slot taken as a model, or old entries left behind). They are now cleaned
  up in full, with new tests that run the game's own cleanup code.
- **Beta 2: the team-combo crash is fixed** (beta 1, hosting alone in the Maintenance Garage): the game makes every
  model from fixed pools, and with one full a team-combo popup lost a hero's head and crashed. Such a popup is now
  hidden (the combo still counts), and **the model pools are four times bigger** in Legends Classic builds
  (`[Engine] ModelPools = x4`: 4096 models, 2048 IGB, 128 actors ...), so effects, props and heads no longer go
  missing in heavy fights. Off by default for plain XML2.
- **Same versions only:** the pools are gameplay-agreement rule 28 (ModelPools) - a full pool refuses spawns - so a
  1.8.0-beta.1 game and a beta.2 Legends Classic game refuse each other at join, naming `28 ModelPools` on the beta.2
  PC. Everyone updates the launcher's XML2 Fix to beta 2 and rebuilds with builder 0.3.0-beta.2.
- **Four-player start is fixed:** participants keep distinct peers when the game starts.
- **Hero swaps survive level changes.** Solo-segment hand-back runs only during the segment and where it ends,
  using the heroes held when the segment began (rule 26 revision 6).
- **Slow players stay connected.** LAG warnings may still show, but the slow-player drop is disabled.
- **Frozen guests:** a joiner frozen for 30 seconds is removed through the agreed cut frame; survivors keep playing.
  A host drop or freeze still ends the joiners' session with "lost connection to the host".

- Host or join online by code, or browse public games, without port forwarding or ini editing. Codes appear in
  the game's online menus and the launcher. Rooms open when you enter Play Online.
- Room tokens are exactly the host's `GameVersion` (`LC33`), so matching public rows can join. The full gameplay
  agreement still checks content and rules on admission.
- Drop-in is **ON by default in Legends Classic 0.3.0**. Start alone; late players wait until the next level change.
  Players without a hero during solo story segments stay connected watching and regain a hero when the party widens.
- When a guest drops abruptly in a game of three or more, survivors agree a cut frame and play on
  (rule 25 GnsTransport revision 4, framing 3). The guest can join again by code. The host keeps hosting after the
  last guest leaves. **Host loss still ends the session; there is no host migration or automatic reconnection.**
- Contract v1.5: `room_closed` means the game cannot take players right now; `room_full` means all four seats are taken.
- Online hardening, encrypted connections and relay fallback. Public games connect directly, so players can see
  each other's internet address. The broker caps each address at two public rooms and two seats in others' rooms.
- Includes 1.7.1's bedroom picture window and per-hit hero stun behavior, and 1.7.0's conversation timing fix.

**Packaging:** the release workflow builds pinned GameNetworkingSockets and links it, protobuf and the project CA
statically into `dinput.dll`. It checks both the built DLL and the zip's DLL with `scripts/check_release_dll.py`;
the DLL imports Windows system libraries only. The zip includes `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.

**Testing limits:** owner 030e acceptance on three real PCs including a hotspot PC is still pending.
Four-player WAN coverage is not certified. Failed shared-campaign restore can require rehosting.
LAG warnings may remain; rule 26 revision 6 disables the slow-player drop. There is no host migration.
