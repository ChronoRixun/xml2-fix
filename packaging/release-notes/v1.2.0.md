- **Display modes.** New, optional `[Display]` section in `xml2-fix.ini`: `Mode=borderless` runs the game in a borderless window at your desktop's resolution and refresh rate (no mode switch, instant alt-tab, keeps running in the background), `Mode=windowed` in a normal centred window, `Mode=fullscreen` keeps exclusive fullscreen but at the desktop's refresh rate. `Width`/`Height` force a resolution; `Topmost` and `RunInBackground` do what they say.
- **Your desktop's resolution in Options → Video.** The game builds its list from the Direct3D 8 modes and has room for 20; the fix adds the desktop resolution and trims the list to what fits.
- **Frame rate and VSync.** The game caps itself at 60 fps with a busy-wait. `[Display] FrameRate=144` (or `refresh` for your desktop's rate, or `0` for no cap) switches that off and paces frames with a high-resolution timer instead, in any mode; the game's speed doesn't change with the frame rate. `VSync=1`/`0` sets the presentation interval in fullscreen; in the borderless and windowed modes, where Direct3D 8 has no usable vsync, `VSync=1` paces frames at the desktop's refresh rate (never above `FrameRate`).
- **For test tooling:** `[Test] InputPipe=1` opens the named pipe `\\.\pipe\xml2-fix-input`, through which a script presses keys and saves screenshots of the frame the game just drew, without the game having the focus; its `status` reply reports the frames per second.
- Without a `[Display]` or `[Test]` section nothing changes: the game runs exactly as with 1.1.1.

Install: copy `dinput.dll` into the folder that contains `XMen2.exe`. See the [README](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/xml2-fix#readme).
