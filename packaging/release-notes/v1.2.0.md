The biggest update yet: display options, Discord presence, button prompts for pads, a mods folder, and the engine features mods and the X-Men Legends I community port need. Drop in the new `dinput.dll`; no settings file needed.

### Heads-up: what changes when you update

- **Some new features are on by default** (list below). Each has a one-line switch in `xml2-fix.ini` next to the DLL.
- **One rule for every `xml2-fix.ini` value.** A value ends at the first `;`, so inline comments work on every key (`Mode=borderless ; my setup`); the spaces around it don't count. An empty value (`Key=` or `Key= ; later`) is the same as no key at all: the default. Switches take `1`/`0`, `true`/`false`, `yes`/`no` or `on`/`off`.
- **`[Online] Domain=off` is now the only way to switch the OpenSpy redirect off.** An empty `Domain=` used to switch it off; it now means the default, `openspy.net`.

### On by default

- **Pad button prompts.** A player on a pad sees the pad's buttons in prompts, the tutorial and the power wheel: **A B X Y** in their Xbox colours, the rest as *[LB]*, *[Start]*. Prompts follow the device each player last used. Off: `[Input] Prompts=off` (or `keyboard`).
- **Discord Rich Presence.** With the Discord app running, your profile shows the zone, your heroes and their levels, and the mode (menus, cutscene, Danger Room, online co-op). No player names, no PC, network or account details; it goes to the Discord app on your PC only. Off: `[Discord] Enabled=0` (`ShowZone=0` / `ShowParty=0` to share less).
- **The right network address on a PC with WSL, Hyper-V, Docker or a VPN.** For online play the game used to take a virtual adapter's address as its own (the *LocalIP* on the Play Online screen), one other players can't reach; it now takes the one this PC reaches the internet from. Old behaviour: `[Online] LocalIP=first`.
- **Four new rows in *Options → Controls → Advanced*:** display mode, frame rate, VSync and run in background. They change nothing until you change them. Hide them: `[Display] InGameOptions=0`.
- **Mods folder.** Put each mod in its own folder under `mods\` next to the game, laid out like the game folder; the game's files stay untouched. `mods\load-order.txt` sets the order and switches mods on (`+Name`) and off (`-Name`). With no files in `mods\`, nothing changes.

### New, when you ask for it

- **Borderless and windowed modes** (`[Display] Mode`) at your desktop's resolution: no mode switch, instant alt-tab. Your desktop's resolution shows in *Options → Video*; `ResolutionList=all` offers up to 64 sizes (and fixes the stock game's overflow of its 20-entry list into the default key bindings).
- **Frame rate above 60 or unlimited** (`FrameRate=144`, `refresh` or `0`) without the game's busy-wait, and **VSync**. Menus stay at 60 fps so pads and keys don't skip items.
- **Bigger zones for big mods.** `[Limits] ActorSlots` (up to 127) and `ResourceNames` (up to 4096) raise the engine's 40-character and 450-name tables, which crash a crowded zone or a long session when full.
- **Online:** `[Online] Server=<IPv4>` sends every GameSpy lookup to one address (a self-hosted OpenSpy); `GameVersion` keeps a mod that is another game apart from XML2's games. The online lobby itself still needs regions on OpenSpy.
- **For mods with their own campaign** (`[Game]`, used by the X-Men Legends I port): `NewGameTeam`, `ResetUnlocks`, `SaveFolder`, `WindowTitle`, `NewGamePlus`, `ForcedTeams` / `AddHero` / `JoinHero` (script functions for fixed parties, with Change Team locked at Xtraction Points), `PostgameScript`, `EndHeroUnlock`, `MainMenuItems`, `ReviewStats`, `XPCurve=xml1`.
- **For test tooling:** `[Test] InputPipe` / `PipeName` / `VirtualPads` (keys, virtual pads, script statements and screenshots over a named pipe, without the focus); `[Debug] LogFiles`.

### Fixed

- The game no longer hangs for seconds at start-up (sometimes long enough for Windows to call it "not responding") while Windows looks for controllers: XInput now starts on a thread of its own.

Every patch checks the bytes it relies on against the retail `XMen2.exe` first; on any other build that feature stays off and `xml2-fix.log` says why. Every setting, with its default: [README → Settings](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/xml2-fix#%EF%B8%8F-settings-xml2-fixini).

**Install:** copy `dinput.dll` into the folder that contains `XMen2.exe` (replace the old one). See the [README](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/xml2-fix#readme).
