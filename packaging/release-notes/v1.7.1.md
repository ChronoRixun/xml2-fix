# XML2 Fix 1.7.1

The engine half of Legends Classic 0.2.5: bedroom item pictures sit in the first
game's picture window, and heroes are re-stunned by every stun hit.

- **Bedroom item pictures:** `[Game] PersonalItemPicture=window` draws a
  personal item's picture as a 256x128 window over the menu's panels, as the
  first game does, instead of stretching it over the whole screen. Absent or
  `stock` keeps the fullscreen picture.
- **Stun per hit:** `[Game] StunRule=xml1` (gameplay rule 18, now revision 3)
  requests the stun reaction for every stun hit on a party hero, as the first
  game does. Before, a hit during a stun, or just after getting up, was ignored.

**Updating Legends Classic:** in Ultimate Legends, **Rebuild**, then **Play**.
Both PCs need builder **0.2.5** and **XML2 Fix 1.7.1**; a peer with rule 18
revision 2 (XML2 Fix 1.7.0) is refused by name. The builder enables both
settings. Launcher 0.3.2 already supports this pair; no launcher release is needed.
The save format is unchanged.
