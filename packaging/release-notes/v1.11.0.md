## 1.11.0-beta.5

XML2 Fix 1.11.0-beta.5 is paired with Legends Classic 0.6.0-beta.6 (content 35 / catalogue 8 / LC45).
Windows file details retain numeric version 1.11.0; the tag and package identify this beta.
Update both parts in Ultimate Legends' **Beta** channel, then **Rebuild**, then **Play**. Beta.1 to beta.5 saves
still load. Everyone in an online session needs the same build and settings; companions older than beta.5 are refused
at join because their weapon contact rule differs (capability lc-npc-beam-trace/2, rule 16 agreement).

**Changes since beta.4**

- **Converted lightning and flame weapons follow the first game's trace timer.** Weapon X lightning officers and
  flamethrower troops share one actor-wide timer, as in the first game, so hits are spaced about 0.6 seconds
  (lightning) and 0.75 seconds (flame) apart instead of arriving much faster.
- **Each weapon contact counts once, as in the first game.** Marked bullet, beam and flame contacts use a private
  copy of the attack with a fresh identity, so the second hit of a burst is no longer swallowed. A full officer burst
  lands about two hits; an MP5 burst can now land both of its hits (2-5 damage each, unchanged). Owen hand-checked the
  lightning officer on November 10: "hit hard, but definitely survivable". The builder's Savage Rampage fix (Legends
  Classic 0.6.0-beta.6) needs no companion change.

**Checks and remaining limits**

Agent checks on the hand-tested candidate measured ten complete officer power bursts (two hits each, at least
0.6 seconds apart), natural officer fire, flame hits on two heroes, an online pair with matching hits on both copies,
and refusal of the older companion in both directions. Not certified: an uninterrupted full flamethrower sweep, an
officer burst ending on its own during natural fire, and the Iceman skating fallback live. An officer near a breakable
object keeps firing at it; the first game does the same. The beta.4 limitations remain in the companion known-issues
page: startup reliability, four-player pause test-input scope, relay/different home networks, three or four separate
PCs, long sessions, slow joins and host loss. Save import from xemu and XML1-recomp remains off.

Back up saves before updating. Standalone builders need the matching DLL in the output folder before Build.
These tools distribute no game content; both owned games are required.

## 1.11.0-beta.2

XML2 Fix 1.11.0-beta.2 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.6.0-beta.2 (content 31 / catalogue 8 /
LC41). Windows file details still show 1.11.0 for the DLL; the package and the release tag carry the beta suffix.
It is a beta: co-op was tested on one PC with up to four copies of the game and on two PCs at home, and still needs
wider testing.

**Install.** Use Ultimate Legends' Beta channel, or copy `dinput.dll` into the Legends Classic game folder (the one
with `XMen2.exe`). For a standalone 0.6.0-beta.2 build, the DLL must be in the output folder **before** Build or
Rebuild: the builder checks it and refuses an older DLL. A 1.11.0-beta.1 game and a 1.11.0-beta.2 game refuse each
other at join.

**For other X-Men Legends II setups.** The classic online screens are written by the Legends Classic builder. Without
its settings the game keeps X-Men Legends II's own online menus. The frame-rate, joining and boss changes below apply
to Legends Classic co-op.

**Fixed since 1.11.0-beta.1**

- **Blob no longer spins and stops attacking.** His first-game brain had no way out of the step where he walks to an
  object to pick up or throw. When he never got there he circled around it and never attacked again, offline and
  online alike, unless a hero kept hitting him. Those steps now end after 5 seconds of game time and his attack
  pattern carries on, at 30 and 60 fps.
- **The first game's other bosses can't get stuck waiting.** The dopples, Avalanche, Shadow King, Magneto and Master
  Mold now give up on a move whose condition never comes true (facing, a power that isn't ready, a tile or waypoint
  they can't reach) after 5 to 10 seconds of game time and choose another. Their successful moves, cooldowns and random
  choices are unchanged. Pyro, Mystique and Marrow are unchanged. The changed bosses have new rule revisions, so
  every player in a session needs this DLL.
- **Host crash with four players fixed.** In 1.11.0-beta.1 a 4-player host could crash when a friend who was joining
  dropped out just as the area reloaded: a late network report from another guest still named the removed player.
  The host now clears that report safely and the other three players carry on.
- **Losing the only friend who is joining no longer stops the game.** The players already in the game stay together
  and keep playing, with no pause screen and no rejoin (tested with two and three players already in the game). At
  the area restart each PC
  now holds a player's input until that player's restart marker arrives (at most 5 seconds), so every PC counts the
  same inputs; a slow guest that hears about the removal early waits for its own copy of that player before removing
  it. Everyone may wait a few seconds at that area load. On by default; no setting is needed.

**What's new**

- **Guests follow the host's frame rate** in code, LAN and IP sessions. The rate is set when the host opens the room
  and stays the same through area loads and late joins. A guest's own frame-rate setting, display and VSync
  preferences stay as they are, in memory and in `xml2-fix.ini`, and the guest gets their own rate back on leaving or
  if a join fails. Browsing rooms doesn't change it. To change the session's rate, leave and host again.
- **The host's online limit still applies.** With the usual `[Online] TickRate=60`, a host set to 144 fps opens a
  60 fps session; a host set to 30 opens a 30 fps session. A slow PC, a stalled connection or fullscreen VSync can
  still run the session below its rate.
- **Player cards show the rates** ("30 local / 60 online") and name a player whose PC can't keep up with the session
  ("Cannot hold 60 fps"). Waiting for another player's input doesn't count as a slow PC, and nothing lowers the rate
  automatically.
- **The classic online screens** cover Game Options, the screen-name and IP keyboards, Other ways, LAN host or join,
  the LAN games list, Connect by IP and the pause menu's player list. X-Men Legends II's own choices, ready states and
  connection steps stay in charge underneath.
- **The pause menu's player list** draws every name, hero and Ready / Not Ready in full, in the player colours, at
  800x600, 1280x960, 1280x720 and 1920x1080 (it was cut into fragments in earlier previews).
- **Keyboard and controller on the new keyboards.** Typed letters, digits and dots, Backspace, arrows / D-pad and
  Enter / A on a key work on the IP and name keyboards. Printable text comes from Windows' own character messages, so
  it follows the keyboard layout (AZERTY, QWERTZ, Shift, Caps Lock, AltGr, dead keys); text the game can't store,
  such as accented letters, shows "Unsupported" instead of a wrong letter. Esc / B, the Cancel button and a mouse
  click on Cancel close the keyboard without changing anything, also on Enter IP (whose field starts empty). Done
  only finishes on Done. Mouse clicks still type once each. The Other ways name keeps its 15-character limit from the
  first frame.
- **Host a game with keyboard and controller.** Up / Down move between rows, Left / Right change the value in place,
  Del / Y and Enter / A toggle DROP-IN, and a held Enter can't also post the game.
- **The analog stick on the online keyboards and Host a game.** Using the analog stick on the online keyboard and Host
  page now moves one key at a time, like the D-pad: one cell (or one Host row) per push, in the direction pushed, on the
  game-name, IP and name keyboards. Holding it repeats like the D-pad (about 0.3 s, then every 0.1 s). On Host a game
  only Up / Down repeat: a held Left / Right, stick or D-pad, changes Visibility or DROP-IN once per push. A push under
  about half-way does nothing, a diagonal follows its stronger direction, and a stick springing back past centre
  doesn't move back. The stick is read from the same controller as the menu's D-pad and buttons. Remapped controllers
  keep their mapping, and movement bound to other keys still works; keyboard, mouse and the game's own menus are
  unchanged.
- **LAN fixes.** Only the host of a game answers LAN searches, so a started game with guests is listed once, and two
  different hosts still show as two rows. Rows read "Player's game (Normal)". A LAN guest's lobby shows the host's game
  name and "LAN" instead of room-code placeholders, and shows rates without inventing a local rate for remote players.
- **Clearer service status.** After a failed request the Play Online screen keeps showing "Unavailable" until a new
  request succeeds; Browse shows a plain message instead of an internal error code; key names are right from the first
  frame; the last game's Rejoin reads F4 on the keyboard and Y on a controller, on the card and in the footer. Join by
  code's footer names its real keys. The lobby's "Waiting for ..." line has normal spacing. The DROP-IN-off refusal
  now says "This session has started. Ask the host to start a new session."
- **If the online screens can't start**, a matching Legends Classic build shows a plain page with Back to the main
  menu and advice to rebuild or update XML2 Fix. It no longer falls back to the GameSpy-era screens.
- **New X-Men Legends artwork** for Discord Rich Presence.

**Known limitations**

- Pause release with four players is verified with test input only: in those runs one player's Ready press
  sometimes didn't register and the pause stayed up until it was pressed again. Two- and three-player pauses
  released every time (two-player pauses also with a real controller on two PCs).
- Hosting alone online: after Ready the game resumes but the pause menu stays open - press B or Esc again (the game's
  own rule for a one-player session).
- Co-op was tested on one PC (up to four copies) and on two PCs at home. Not verified yet: the
  relay (networks where a direct connection fails), players on different home networks, three or four separate PCs,
  long sessions and joins over slow connections.
- When a friend who is joining drops out during the admission, the host can show the game's own "lost online sync"
  message a few times. It is cosmetic: the session carries on and nobody else is dropped.
- If the host leaves, the session ends: no host migration or automatic reconnection yet (planned for 0.8).
- Enter IP's keyboard still draws its unused cells and two empty bottom buttons as boxes (cosmetic).
- With a working service the Play Online screen's status can read "Connecting..." for a long time; code joins work.
- The new screens were checked with a real controller and keyboard on two PCs; more controllers and resolutions are
  still to come. Save import is not switched on for players.

**How to update**

Back up `SaveLC`. Update Ultimate Legends, then in the **Beta** channel install **XML2 Fix 1.11.0-beta.2** and the
**Legends Classic builder 0.6.0-beta.2**, **Rebuild**, then **Play**. Everyone needs the same pair and the same
gameplay settings. Standalone builds: copy `dinput.dll` into the output folder before rebuilding.

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. The package contains the fix and its documentation; no game files,
game art or modified game executable.

## 1.11.0-beta.1

XML2 Fix 1.11.0-beta.1 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.6.0-beta.1 (content 30 / catalogue 8 / LC40).
Windows file details show 1.11.0 for the DLL; the package and the release tag carry the beta suffix. It is a beta:
the co-op parts were tested on one PC with two copies of the game and on two PCs at home, and still need wider
testing (see "Known limitations"). Guard stays off by default.

**Install.** Use Ultimate Legends' Beta channel, or copy `dinput.dll` into the Legends Classic game folder (the one
with `XMen2.exe`). For a standalone 0.6.0-beta.1 build, the DLL must be in the output folder **before** Build or
Rebuild: the default co-op screens check it while the build runs. A 1.10 game and a 1.11 game refuse each other at
join.

**For other X-Men Legends II setups.** The new screens, fonts and joining a game in progress are written by the
Legends Classic builder (`[Online] Menus=1`, `[Online] LateJoin=1`). Without those settings the game keeps
X-Men Legends II's own online menus and joins only in the lobby, and the controller, display, Discord and mod features work as before.

**What's new: online co-op inside the game**

- **Play Online** now opens the game's own co-op screens. **Host** opens a room: choose Public or Private, give it a
  name, pick a new or saved campaign and choose whether friends may join after the start (**DROP-IN**). The room code
  appears as soon as the room is open, with **Copy code**. **Join by code** takes a pasted or typed code, and
  **Browse** lists public rooms with the host, campaign, player count and drop-in setting.
- **The lobby** shows everyone who has joined. Players mark themselves Ready and the host starts when the group is
  set. The host can also start alone.
- **The Party screen** (the pause menu's Party row) shows who is playing and what each player is doing, keeps the
  room code at hand for inviting more friends, and lets the host remove a player.
- **Other ways** still opens X-Men Legends II's original online menus (LAN search, Connect by IP). The new screens and
  their fonts are on by default; build with `--online-menus off` to keep the original menus.
- Clear messages explain every connection problem on the screen where it happens, with **Retry** only where trying
  again can help. When a room or player limit is reached, the message says what to do instead.

**What's new: join a game in progress**

- **Friends can join a game that has already started.** The newcomer waits on Joined Game while the others keep
  playing; at the host's next area load everyone, the host included, reloads that area together and the newcomer
  takes a free hero. That one load takes a little longer. Nobody appears in the middle of an area.
- Joining a game in progress works with two, three and four players, at any exit and in areas with enemies: a
  second or third friend joins a running game the same way, and two friends who are waiting at the same time both
  come in at the same exit.
- When a friend joins, the host and the other players wait up to 10 seconds on an "A player is joining..." message
  instead of replaying a moment of play. The wait is `[Online] JoinHoldSeconds` in `xml2-fix.ini` (default 10, 0..15;
  `0` turns it off); each PC uses its own value. If a join takes longer than the wait, play continues and the
  friend comes in moments later.
- If two friends are joining and one of them drops out, the other still gets in and everyone plays on: the host
  decides once and every PC goes on without the friend who dropped. If the host drops out while a friend is
  joining, the others see the host-left message.
- Joining a game in progress is on by default. The host's **DROP-IN** setting turns it off for one room; a friend
  who enters the code of a game that has started then is told that the session has started and to ask the host to
  return to the lobby. A player who set `LateJoin=0` in `xml2-fix.ini` keeps that choice through a rebuild or repair.
- In testing, admissions on one PC and on two PCs at home held through movement by both players and the walked exits
  that followed, with every PC staying in step.

**Co-op fixes since 0.5**

- A lost connection to the room listing service no longer ends a healthy game during a long loading screen: players
  already in keep playing, and new players cannot join until the host opens a new room.
- When the host quits (even with a dead hero), crashes or loses the connection, guests see a host-left message with
  working **Host again** and **Back to Online** buttons. A sudden crash can take about ten seconds to be noticed.
- Guests stay connected while the host uses Team Management, and a player who leaves and rejoins gets the roster back.
- Players can now leave a lobby or a game and others can still join: before this, after someone other than the most
  recent player left, a new or returning player could lose the connection a minute after joining, taking another
  player with them. In testing on two PCs with four players, after a player left the lobby (from the first, a middle
  or the last place) or quit a game in progress, a returning or brand-new player joined, in the lobby or later in the
  game, and everyone stayed connected and in step.
  With three or four players, the guests now connect to each other the same way they connect to the host, and when
  one player's game closes suddenly, the others agree that it left and play on.
- Fixed a crash when starting with a guest, focus and input getting stuck when returning to Play Online, and
  Party confirmations that swallowed the next Quit or Back.
- Screens free their text, panels and model data when they close; repeated opening and closing kept memory use steady
  in automated checks.
- Ready, Start, lobby cards, badges, headings, prompts and the Copy code button were corrected; Browse, Retry and the
  dialogs respond correctly to keyboard, controller and clicks, including on button edges and corners. Resolution keeps
  its value at 4:3.
- Relay-only connections refuse an unavailable relay instead of quietly connecting directly, and refusing one
  connection no longer disturbs the healthy players in the room.
- The flying carry attaches the carried character from the first frame.

**Other fixes**

- Steam Deck and other Linux PCs that run the game through Proton no longer stop on a black screen at start: every
  XML2 Fix since 1.8.1 could wait there forever while it looked for controllers. Under Proton (Wine),
  `[Input] CacheDeviceScan` now defaults to `0`, so controllers are found the way they were before 1.8.1; a setting
  written in `xml2-fix.ini` is used as written, and Windows is unchanged.

**Carried forward from 0.5**

- **The first game's items, shops and drops.** All 116 X-Men Legends equipment pieces with their bonuses, the first
  game's drop tables, the Forge tier by tier, the Healer's prices, the Grab Bag, selling and Danger Room rewards. Where
  a bonus has no exact PC equivalent it is converted as closely as the PC game allows; the build's
  `Docs\APPROXIMATIONS.md` lists each one.
- **A bigger save format** with room for 1024 item definitions and 4096 item bonus records, more objectives, talents
  and world items. Existing saves load and are converted.
- **Phoenix's telekinesis** (hold power 1 to lift, move and throw an enemy) and **the flying carry** (Flight rank 3
  for Phoenix, Rogue or Storm: hold Guard over an ally or enemy to pick them up, carry them for about 10 seconds and
  throw them).
- **Guard (Block / Grab) works as in beta 8.** The first game's grab rules and held blocking stay optional
  (`--guard-decide xml1`) and off by default until they pass real-controller checks; `docs/RELEASE_GUARD.md`
  describes how that default would change.

These stay on by default, from the launcher and from the command line alike; options you turn off stay off.

**How to update.** Use Ultimate Legends' Beta channel: update **XML2 Fix to 1.11.0-beta.1** and **the builder to
0.6.0-beta.1**, then **Rebuild**, then **Play**. With the standalone builder, copy the matching `dinput.dll` into the
output folder before you build (the co-op screens need it during the build).

**Co-op needs the same version.** Everyone needs the same pair, content version 30 and the same gameplay settings,
with Test mode off. A 0.5 game (content version 29) cannot join a 0.6 game. The item catalogue is unchanged from 0.5.

**Back up your saves.** Copy `Documents\Activision\X-Men Legends\SaveLC` somewhere safe before updating. 0.6 keeps the
0.5 save format; beta 8 and stable 0.4.0 cannot load these saves (they refuse them without changing them).

- Joining a game in progress happens only at the host's next area, never mid-area.
