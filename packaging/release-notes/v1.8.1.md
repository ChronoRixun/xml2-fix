XML2 Fix 1.8.1 is a hotfix for 1.8.0, the engine half of Legends Classic 0.4.0 (content 27, lobby token `LC36`),
with Ultimate Legends launcher 0.4.0.

- **No more pause about once a second with a controller connected.** The game re-checks its controllers about once
  a second, and on many PCs that check is slow enough to show as a short pause (easiest to see on the Team
  Management screen's clouds). XML2 Fix now does the check in the background and hands the game the last complete
  list, and finds XInput pads on its own thread instead of asking every pad slot on every read. Plugging a pad in
  or out is still picked up. `[Input] CacheDeviceScan = 0` in `xml2-fix.ini` switches
  back to the old checks ([details](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/xml2-fix/blob/v1.8.1/docs/controller-scan-cache.md)).

**How to update.** In Ultimate Legends (Stable channel), update **XML2 Fix to 1.8.1** and **Play**. No Rebuild is
needed and the builder stays 0.4.0. Saves carry over; the save format is unchanged.

**Co-op:** 1.8.1 changes no gameplay rule, so a 1.8.1 PC and a 1.8.0 PC play together (the same Legends Classic 0.4.0
build on both). Everything else is 1.8.0's, including its known issues
([1.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/ChronoRixun/xml2-fix/releases/tag/v1.8.0)).

**Packaging:** as 1.8.0 - pinned GameNetworkingSockets, protobuf and the project CA linked statically into
`dinput.dll`, checked by `scripts/check_release_dll.py`; Windows system imports only.
