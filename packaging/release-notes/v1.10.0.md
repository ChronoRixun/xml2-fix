XML2 Fix 1.10.0 is the engine half of Legends Classic 0.5.0 (the 0.4.2 line released as 0.5: content 29, lobby token
`LC38`), with Ultimate Legends launcher 0.4.0 or later. It needs the matching builder: update XML2 Fix to
1.10.0-beta.1 and the builder to 0.5.0-beta.1, **Rebuild**, then **Play**. A 1.10.0 game and a 1.9.0 game (beta 8) or
1.8.x game (stable) refuse each other at join, so everyone in a co-op game needs the same pair.

**Back up your saves.** The first time 0.5 saves your campaign it writes a bigger save format. Beta 8 and the stable
0.4.0 cannot load a save written by 0.5 (they refuse it and do not change it). If you might go back, copy
`Documents\Activision\X-Men Legends\SaveLC` somewhere safe before you play. Older saves load in 0.5.

**What's new in 1.10.0-beta.1**

- **The first game's items, shops and drops** (gameplay rule 52). All 116 X-Men Legends equipment pieces with their
  bonuses, the first game's drop tables, the Forge and the Healer with the first game's stock and prices, selling, the
  Grab Bag and the Danger Room rewards.
- **A bigger save format** (W2, gameplay rule 47): 1024 item definitions instead of 160, 4096 item bonus records
  instead of 512, and more objectives, talents, missions and world items per save - room for the new items and for
  heavier mods.
- **Phoenix's telekinesis from the first game:** hold power 1 to lift an enemy, move him and throw him; he takes
  damage from what he hits.
- **Guard (Block / Grab) works as in beta 8.** The first game's Guard rule is in this DLL but held back for a later
  beta: on a real controller it refused grabs the game would make and showed no block, so the 0.5 builder leaves it off.
- **The flying carry:** with Flight rank 3, Phoenix, Rogue and Storm fly over an ally or an enemy, hold Guard to come
  down and pick him up, carry him (about 10 seconds) and throw him.
- **Controllers connected after the game started now work in the game.** `[Input] LatePads = 0` in `xml2-fix.ini`
  switches back to the old behaviour.
- **More engine headroom** (limits wave 3, rules 53-62): fighting styles, style events, fight moves, effect templates,
  primitives, effect vectors, script pools, particle scratch, attached powerups and callback nodes.
- **Co-op:** a player still choosing a hero shows as `(Spectating)`. When the host's gameplay settings differ from
  yours, your games list now says so ("Host's gameplay settings differ: ...") and a message tells you that both players
  need the same build and the same settings in `xml2-fix.ini` (before, the list only said "No Games Found").
- **Clearer save messages:** a save from a newer build is refused with a clear message and left untouched, and a save
  made with the optional 96-actor limit on now tells you to turn `Actors=96` back on to load it (instead of asking for
  an update).

**Off by default:** the 96-live-actor limit (`[Limits] Actors=96`, with `[Limits] ActorPools=124`) is built in but off;
the builder does not write it. A save written with it on needs it on to load (0.5 with it off asks you to turn it on;
beta 8 and 0.4.0 refuse it), so back up first if you try it.

**Tested:** automated runs on the 0.4.2 candidates (082c-082f) covered the new save format on seed saves and a copy of a
real campaign, the shops, buying, selling and the Grab Bag, drop rates per roll against the first game's, heavy fights
with the new limits, telekinesis on a soldier you face (10 of 10), the flying pickup (4 of 4) and a 10-second carry, three boots to the main menu, and co-op
on one PC with two instances (join, fight, level changes, a save on both; an older build or different settings refused
on both sides). The optional 96-actor limit ran 96 live actors in a boss fight, refused the 97th cleanly, and its saves
reloaded. The two new messages above passed automated checks but have not been seen in a live game yet. Play between
separate PCs has not been tested on 1.10.0 yet.

**Known issues**

- Phoenix's telekinesis lifts the enemy she faces; when she faces away or stands in a crowd it can pick a crate next to
  him, or nobody (6 of 10 in that test).
- A flying carry ends when the carried one bumps a prop or a wall, or when the carrier lands (fly higher over crates).
- Flying carry: at the pickup the carried hero is invisible for a moment, then appears in the carrier's hands
  (visual only).
- Throws: a thrown enemy is hurt only by what he hits. In testing, "Physical Resistant" soldiers lost 4 of 100 health
  from an Attack throw and none from a Smash throw; whether that is enough is still being judged, and a throw straight
  into a wall has not been tested yet.
- The first game's Guard (stunned pickups, Beast's wall lunge) comes in a later beta; Guard is beta 8's for now.
- From beta 8 and 0.4.0: Blob can rarely stand still; at mid range the MP5 soldier still does more damage than the
  original; button prompts (#8).

Report bugs in the community Discord's #beta-bugs channel with the level, the characters and `xml2-fix.log`.
